03 / About

About

I am drawn to problems where the analysis and the execution cannot be separated. A market entry recommendation that nobody can implement is an expensive document. A tool nobody asked for is a hobby. The work I care about sits between them.

That is most of what I do now. At Daybreak I scoped the strategy and then built the thing. In a franchise growth role I mapped three years of order data and then built the outreach around what it said. I started a lawn care company at twelve because I wanted to see whether the whole loop, pricing through retention, would close.

I am heading toward management consulting, and eventually toward work in geopolitics and the Middle East, where I have read seriously for years. Outside of that I ski, I cook, and I speak fluent Spanish.
